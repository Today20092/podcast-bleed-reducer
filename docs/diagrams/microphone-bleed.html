Text equivalent and example settings

Speaker 3 is speaking and Speaker 1 is silent. Microphone 3 records direct speech. Microphone 1 records leakage from the same speaker plus room noise. The synchronized illustrative envelopes share a time and amplitude scale. Their relative amplitudes are invented for teaching, not calibrated measurements or thresholds.

Label everyone speaking: Speaker 3 only. This supplies both direct-speech and bleed examples. The desired action is to preserve track 3 and attenuate track 1 when its assigned person is absent. Loudness or detected speech alone cannot prove track ownership. Gain reduction lowers the whole track in selected intervals; it does not subtract a separated voice.
